* Emulated Operator Printer.
* Added the NSM DATAprint 3000 as a selectable 24-column impact-printer model.
* Moved printer selection to **Tools > Printer type** and persist it as the
  `[Photo Play] printer` INI option (`dpu414` or `dataprint3000`).
* Added a persistent **DATAprint 3000 in English** option which translates the
  top and keyboard legends, card label, and all firmware-generated messages.
* Rebuilt the DATAprint 3000 simulator from the documented 113 x 230 mm case and
  57 mm paper dimensions, with the real lamp order, paper direction, case-edge
  controls, three-button setup keyboard, SRAM card, paper-out and transfer-error
  states, manual print/delete/initialise workflows, one-level deleted-data
  recovery, full-versus-faulted card states, and manual paper-out restart.
* Split persistent DATAprint-owned parameters from removable SRAM-card settings,
  added clock-relative timestamps and running data-set identities, and made VDAI
  storage transactional: checksum-invalid, reset or unplugged reports are never
  committed. Busy Photo Play polling now reveals the unplugged unit so the
  connection and post-completion removal sequence remains visible to the user.
* Added a distinct procedural sound model for the DATAprint 3000's Epson M-160:
  four-solenoid impact chatter, lead-cam motor, ribbon and paper-feed mechanics,
  57 mm paper tearing, physical controls, and the documented transfer, complete,
  external-error and internal-error beeper cadences.
* Made both printer simulator windows follow Windows display scaling, including
  high-resolution painted canvases and correctly aligned hardware hit targets.
* Made each printer window follow its real paper path: the DPU-414 keeps its
  controls below a roll that grows upward, while the DATAprint 3000 keeps its
  controls at the top and lets its operator-facing receipt grow downward to the
  desktop work-area limit before scrolling.
