* Ethernet: the cabinet has an RTL8139 now, and an image given the Ethernet option (`tools/ethernet-patch`, all Photo Play 2001 and I.G.O. 1–8 builds) reaches fun.net through it instead of the modem — a *connection type* field in the service menu's dial-in settings, Modem or Ethernet.
* Remote Switch: point the card at a fun.net stand-in anywhere on the internet (`host:port`, optional shared secret); works behind home NAT.
* The machine licence is a setting: `[Photo Play] machlic = random | pool:N | <12 hex digits>`, so cabinets stop all being `00584F425050`.
* The modem paces its receive side by the character and honours its output ring, so fun.net transfers over the modem no longer stall on retransmissions.
